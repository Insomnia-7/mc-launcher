## 最终生成提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background image for the very wide shallow hero banner of a Minecraft desktop launcher. Generate an authentic Minecraft survival world screenshot with beautiful realistic shader lighting, recognizable voxel grass blocks, stepped green hills and blocky leafy trees, a glowing purple Nether portal on the RIGHT third, sunset lavender and pink sky, distant misty mountains and a calm valley. Extremely wide short horizontal panorama, approximately 8:1 aspect ratio, composed for a shallow banner crop. All key landscape features and the portal should fit inside the middle horizontal 25 percent of the image height if a taller canvas is necessary. Left half is quiet, low detail, dark indigo-purple sky and distant valley suitable for white text overlay added later in the application. Right half rich green hills and warm peach rim light, portal visible in full. Style: actual in-game Minecraft screenshot with high quality survival shaders, soft bloom, ambient occlusion, atmospheric depth, crisp cubic voxel geometry and pixel textures; not rounded plastic toys, not painted concept art, not photoreal non-voxel geography. Color palette: indigo, lavender, pink sunset and muted green. No text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no border, no collage, no red marks, no characters, no HUD. Render only the scenery as one continuous panorama.
